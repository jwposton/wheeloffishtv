<objective>
Make catalog sync the primary qualifying evidence channel (D-02/D-03) and the safety reset gate (D-04). After `run_chunked_sync` completes a successful full sync and purges stale `CachedSeries`, increment absence evidence for playlist rows whose series is now absent, clear prune state for recovered series (D-11), and run a connection-scoped auto-prune (D-06). On any failed, unauthorized, or stalled sync, reset all candidate counters to 0 (D-04) so a transient outage can never drive deletion.

Purpose: The `CachedSeries` purge at the end of `run_chunked_sync` (lines 554-559) is the authoritative "confident absence" signal — series the provider no longer returns are deleted from cache. This plan turns that signal into safe, policy-gated evidence.
Output: Sync-completion and failure-path wiring in `catalog_sync.py` + integration tests in a new `test_catalog_sync_prune.py`.
</objective>
